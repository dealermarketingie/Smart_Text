# Smart Text — content rules

Standing rules for all copy on this site (homepage, industry pages, agent finder, FAQs, docs). Apply these to every future edit, not just the ones already fixed.

## Product accuracy

- **No two-way messaging.** Smart Text is a database reactivation / customer engagement tool for one-to-many outbound messaging. It is not live chat and not a two-way conversation tool like WhatsApp Business. Never describe replies as a conversation; if a use case needs a reply, describe it as a linked action (confirm, book, reschedule) or as replies being routed to staff, not as back-and-forth texting.
- **Scheduling, not automation.** Smart Text supports scheduled sends. It does not do trigger-based automation or automated workflows. Avoid "automatically," "automated," and "automation" in product claims. Use "scheduled," "schedule," or describe the manual trigger (e.g. "once uploaded, you can schedule reminders").
- **CRM integration: yes.** Smart Text can connect to a business's CRM.
- **WhatsApp integration: yes**, for lead nurturing.
- **UK / Ireland / EU market only.** No US-specific systems or compliance claims: no HIPAA, no DMS dependency claims, no MLS, no PMS assumptions. GDPR-friendly, not US privacy law.
- Unverified integrations (property portal sync, Shopify/WooCommerce, PMS) should not be claimed until confirmed. Flag them rather than guessing.

## Voice and formatting

- **No em dashes.** Use a period, comma, or colon instead. Split into two sentences if needed.
- Avoid generic AI-sounding filler phrasing. Write plainly and specifically rather than in vague marketing-speak.
- "Smart Text" is always two words, always capitalized as a product name.
